How to use this test

Short guide for friends trying ClinicPacks. Take ~5–10 minutes, then tell Sam what felt clear or confusing.

What this is

An NZ clinic education pilot — patient-facing packs a physio might share after a visit. Harbour Physio Grey Lynn branding is demo only.

Not a diagnosis. Education and safety screening only. Frozen Research copy for P01 and P05.

How to navigate

  1. Landing — pick a pack (P01 or P05).
  2. Splash — pack title; tap Continue.
  3. Shared — “from your clinician” context; start the safety check.
  4. Safety check — answer each red-flag Yes / No.
  5. Hard stop — if any Yes → urgent guidance (111, ED, Healthline, clinic phone).
  6. Results — if all No → education, self-care, who helps in NZ.

What to try

  • Tick one red flag Yes (then continue) — you should see the hard-stop screen.
  • Go back and answer none of these / all No — you should reach results.
  • Try both P01 (irritable low back) and P05 (leg-dominant / sciatica-style education).

What feedback Sam wants

  • Trust — does this feel safe and credible for a clinic to share?
  • Wording — anything unclear, alarming, or too clinical?
  • Would a clinic pay? — rough gut feel for value to NZ physio clinics.
  • Confusing bits — navigation, buttons, or where you got stuck.

Message Sam directly with notes — no in-app form in this pilot.

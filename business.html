ClinicPacks — what it is & how it makes money

Plain-English overview for NZ physio friends. Education product — not a diagnosis service.

1 · What it means (simple)

In one breath

  • Clinics brand short patient education packs (MSK / ACC-safe info).
  • Patients get calm “what this might relate to” + a red-flag safety check + NZ trusted links.
  • Community gets better info; clinics look professional between visits.

What it is not

  • Not a diagnosis app
  • Not a HEP / exercise video library
  • Not a practice management system (PMS)

4 · How it stays sustainable

Why this can last

  • Recurring subscription — not one-off projects.
  • Low marginal cost — same packs, many clinics (white-label).
  • Content governance — research-frozen education; NZ-licensed physio friends / academic input.
  • Stay narrow — education + red flags + assign/track. Don’t clone Physitrack or Cliniko.
  • Growth path — more packs → more clinics → ACC / employer later.
  • Costs — hosting, clinical review, sales/support.
SaaS recurring White-label packs Narrow scope
5 · Why now

Timing

  • Digital MSK care is growing; NZ ACC education / telehealth tailwinds.
  • Clinics already buy tools — we are a cheaper education add-on, not another full stack.
